Clickety support

Clickety is a row counter for knitting, crochet, and sewing on iPhone and iPad. The whole screen is the button, so you can tap without looking. One project is included. A one-time Unlock purchase adds unlimited projects, linked row and repeat counters, and row alerts. There are no ads, no account, and no subscription.

Contact

Need help? Email hallanhype@gmail.com. I'm a solo developer and usually reply within a few days. If something isn't working, it helps to include your device and the app version, which is on the About screen in Settings.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore the Unlock purchase?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy Unlock. In Clickety, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. You can also tap Restore purchases on the Unlock screen.

If Clickety says no previous unlock was found for this Apple ID, make sure you're signed in with the Apple ID that made the purchase, then try again. Restore from a backup file, also in Settings, is for project data you saved to Files, not for the Unlock purchase.

For a refund, contact Apple. Their refund policy applies.

How do row counting and repeats work?

On the counter screen, tap anywhere to add one to your Row count. −1 and Undo sit apart from the tap area, so a stray tap is easy to fix.

Linked row and repeat counters are part of Unlock. Open a project, tap Counters, then Repeat every N rows (for example, 8). Your Row count keeps going, Pattern row cycles from 1 to N, and Repeats goes up when Pattern row wraps back to 1. You can also add an Independent counter for stitches, decreases, or anything else you want to tally separately.

How do I change haptics and sound?

Open Settings. Haptics is on by default and gives a soft tick on every row. Tap sound is off unless you turn it on. Use the Haptics and Tap sound switches to change either one.
