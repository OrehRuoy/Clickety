Clickety privacy

Clickety has no account, no ads, and no tracking. Your projects stay on your device, and in backup files you choose to save. Purchases are handled by Apple.

If you answer "No" to "Are you enjoying Clickety?" and tap Send, Clickety sends that note, the app version, and the device system to Web3Forms so the developer can read it. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.
